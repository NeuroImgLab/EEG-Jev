      Choice The five stages

      • WAwake. Fast, low-voltage activity; alpha rhythm (8–12 Hz) when the eyes are closed.
      • N1Drifting off. The lightest sleep; alpha fades into slower theta (4–7 Hz).
      • N2Stable light sleep. Marked by sleep spindles: short 11–16 Hz bursts.
      • N3Deep, slow-wave sleep. Large slow waves below 2 Hz.
      • REMDreaming sleep. Low-voltage, mixed-frequency activity that resembles wakefulness.

      Noul What the number means

      Model P(seizure overlap) is the model’s probability that an annotated seizure overlaps this 5-second window. The dataset label is “seizure overlap” when the window and an annotated seizure interval share any time at all; otherwise no annotated seizure overlaps this 5-second window. Neither is the share of the window that is seizure.

      The marked threshold was fixed on development data.

      Choice What the options are

      The question lists the motor-imagery tasks of this experiment, and the model returns one probability for each. The label is the task the participant was cued to perform in the trial.

        Score How to read the distribution

        Each bar is the probability the model gives to one rating from 0 to 10. The teal marker is the expected value, the sum of each rating times its probability. The amber marker is the rating the participant reported for this laser stimulus.

          How it works

          EEG and a question in, a typed answer out

          A frozen REVE-L encoder turns the EEG window into features, and a trainable MLP connector maps them into the embedding space of Qwen3.5-2B, whose frozen base weights are adapted by LoRA. The EEG, the question and the answer specification pass through the model once. At a learned readout marker, the readout selected by the answer type returns probabilities; no text is generated.

          • ChoiceWhich of the listed candidates?A probability for every candidate. Here: sleep stage, motor imagery.
          • NoulDoes the proposition hold?One probability. Here: does an annotated seizure overlap this 5-s window.
          • ScoreWhat value on the specified scale?A distribution over task-specified numerical values and their expected value. Pain example · 0–10.
